## What to look for

- Type in one note. The other note does not change: each `Provider` has its own store.
- The second note starts from a title supplied through the `value` prop.
- Editing a title renders the title field only. The body and the word count keep their counts.
- **Remove last note** throws that store away. **Add a note** creates a fresh one.
